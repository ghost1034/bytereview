The **AI Assistant** is a floating chat that follows you across the Analytics modules. Unlike the [research bots](/docs/ai-analytics-suite/research-bots), it's **context-aware**: it knows which module you're in and can see the data on your screen, so its answers are specific to what you're actually working on.

## Opening and moving it

Look for the assistant launcher (a chat icon) on any Analytics page. Click it to open the chat; it greets you with the current module, for example *"I see you're in the Variance & Flux Analysis module."* You can:

- **Minimize / maximize** the window to keep it out of the way.
- **Drag** it to reposition it on screen.
- **Close** it — it keeps your conversation while you navigate.
- Watch for an **unread badge** when a reply arrives while it's minimized.

## Asking for help

Type into the box (*"Ask anything about accounting…"*) and send. Because the assistant sees your current screen, you can ask about specific accounts, balances, or items in front of you. When you open it, it also offers **suggested prompts** tailored to the module you're in:

| Module | Example prompts |
| --- | --- |
| **Variance** | *"Explain the top 5 material variances"* · *"What's the appropriate materiality threshold for this dataset?"* · *"Draft a variance analysis memo for the reviewer"* |
| **Reconciliation** | *"Why are these transactions unmatched?"* · *"Suggest matches for the remaining unmatched items"* · *"How should I categorize this exception?"* |
| **Fixed Assets** | *"Is this the correct amortization method for this asset type?"* · *"Explain the GAAP vs. Tax difference for this asset"* · *"What journal entries do I need for this month?"* |
| **Waterfall** | *"How much revenue will we recognize next month?"* · *"Is this the correct recognition method under ASC 606?"* · *"Show me the remaining performance obligations"* |
| **Dashboard** | *"Summarize my team's activity this week"* · *"What items are pending my review?"* |

## A reconciliation superpower

When you're in Reconciliation and ask the assistant about your unmatched items, it can do more than explain — it can **propose a new matching pass** and offer to add it directly to your reconciliation, so you can re-run the match with the improved rule.

## Exporting a conversation

You can keep a record of any conversation. **Copy** a message, or export the whole transcript as **PDF**, **Word (.docx)**, or **Excel** for your workpapers.

> **Note:** The assistant is meant for quick, in-context help and isn't saved as a permanent record the way research sessions are. For durable, citation-heavy research you'll want to refer back to, use the [IRS & GAAP Researchers](/docs/ai-analytics-suite/research-bots).
