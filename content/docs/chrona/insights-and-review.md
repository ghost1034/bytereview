Beyond the raw timeline, Chrona helps you make sense of your time. This page (for **staff**) covers the in-app **Dashboard** for trends and breakdowns, and the **Review** workflow for rating how you spent each block.

## The in-app Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** from the top toolbar for an at-a-glance view of where your time went.

### Choose a range

At the top, set the **Range** you want to analyze:

- **Selected day**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Custom** (which reveals **Start** and **End** date pickers).
- Toggle **Include System cards** to include or exclude automatic System activity.

### Key metrics

Four cards summarize the range:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Tracked** | Total time Chrona captured as activity. |
| **Untracked** | Time in the range with no cards (gaps). |
| **Review coverage** | The share of your tracked time you've rated in Review. |
| **Longest Work block** | Your longest uninterrupted block in the Work category. |

### Breakdown panels

Below the metrics, the dashboard shows:

- **Category breakdown** — each category's total time, with a colored bar showing its share of the range.
- **Daily totals** — one stacked bar per day, colored by category. Click any day to open it in the Timeline.
- **Review** — your **Focus**, **Neutral**, and **Distracted** totals, how much of your tracked (non-System) time you've covered, and how many cards are still unreviewed.
- **Top titles** — the activities you spent the most time on.

> **Note:** If there's nothing to show, the dashboard reads "No dashboard data" — generate timeline cards first (record and let analysis run) or pick a different range.

## The Review workflow

Review lets you add your own judgment on top of what Chrona recorded, rating each block as **focused**, **neutral**, or **distracted**. Over time this builds a picture of how your time actually felt, not just what you did.

To review your day:

1. Open **Review** from the top toolbar (it shows the currently selected day).
2. Chrona lists the cards that still need attention, with the goal: **rate cards until coverage reaches 80%**. Each row shows the card's title, time range, category, and how much of it is already covered.
3. For each card, click **Focus**, **Neutral**, or **Distracted**.
4. Coverage updates as you go. Once everything is at least 80% covered, you'll see **"Nothing to review."**

> **Tip:** Your review ratings feed the Dashboard's Review panel and can be folded into the [Ask chat and journal drafts](/docs/chrona/ask-and-journal), so a few minutes of rating makes those features noticeably sharper.
