TaxAtlas is CPAAutomation's source-backed global tax intelligence workspace. It brings tax rates, regulations, court decisions, tariff measures, and detected changes together so tax, finance, and legal teams can monitor jurisdictions without maintaining a collection of disconnected trackers.

## What you can do

- **Explore the map** — compare current rate data geographically and open a jurisdiction for more detail.
- **Research jurisdictions** — review country and sub-national profiles, current and historical rates, and related legal material.
- **Monitor change** — follow a single feed of newly detected rate, regulation, court, and tariff updates.
- **Verify the source** — use confidence markers, effective and as-of dates, and direct source links before relying on a figure.
- **Create watchlists** — follow the jurisdictions and change types that matter to your work.
- **Connect workflows** — export data, create API keys, and configure email or webhook delivery channels.

## Where to find it

Open **TaxAtlas** from the dashboard's **Products** group or go directly to `/dashboard/taxatlas`. TaxAtlas is available on paid plans.

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | Geographic comparison of tax-rate and activity overlays. |
| **Overview** | Coverage statistics, crawl freshness, and recent changes. |
| **Jurisdictions** | Searchable country and sub-national tax profiles. |
| **Regulations** | Tax rules, effective dates, status, and source material. |
| **Courts** | Tax-related decisions, outcomes, and significance. |

## Start with the map

TaxAtlas opens on the **Map**. Choose a metric, pan or zoom to the region you want to study, and select a jurisdiction to open its detail panel. From there you can review rates, regulations, decisions, tariffs, and recent changes without losing your place on the map.

Use **Overview** when you want a coverage summary or a quick read of the newest changes. Use the dedicated list pages when you need filters, sorting, CSV export, or a larger research result set.

## Data and professional judgment

TaxAtlas aggregates public information for monitoring and research. It is not tax, legal, or trade advice. Check the confidence marker, effective or as-of date, and linked primary authority before using a figure in a filing, position, or client recommendation.

## API and notifications

The **Account** area contains API keys, watchlists, notifications, and delivery channels. Paid users can create read keys for TaxAtlas data. Administrative keys and global data changes require system-administrator permission. Webhook deliveries are signed so receiving systems can verify that a payload came from TaxAtlas.
