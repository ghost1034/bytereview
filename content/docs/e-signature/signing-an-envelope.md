---
title: "Signing an Envelope"
description: "Open a signature request, consent to electronic records, complete assigned fields, or decline to sign."
order: 3
---

You can open an envelope from the link in its signature-request email or from **E-Signature > Awaiting my signature** in CPAAutomation.

## Before you begin

Sign in to a CPAAutomation account that uses the exact email address invited by the sender. If you do not have an account for that address, create one from the sign-in flow. CPAAutomation may also require phone verification as part of the identity evidence recorded for the signing process.

For sequential envelopes, the inbox can show **Waiting on others**. The **Review & sign** button becomes available when all signers in earlier routing orders have finished.

## Step 1: Review the disclosure

Opening an eligible request starts the signing session. Before interacting with the document, review the **Electronic records and signatures** disclosure.

- Click **I agree to use electronic records and signatures** to record your consent and continue.
- Click **Decline to sign** if you do not want to use the electronic process.

The documents remain unavailable for interaction until consent is recorded.

## Step 2: Adopt your signature

Click **Adopt signature** or select a signature field. In the dialog, choose one method:

- **Type** - enter your name and preview it in the signature style.
- **Draw** - draw your signature with a mouse, trackpad, touchscreen, or pointer.

Click **Adopt and Sign**. The adopted signature is applied to every signature field assigned to you in the envelope. Initials fields use initials generated from your recipient name.

## Step 3: Complete your fields

Work through the highlighted fields assigned to you:

- Signature and initials fields use the signature you adopted.
- **Date signed** fields are completed automatically.
- Enter a value in each required text field.
- Select each required checkbox.

The action bar shows how many required fields are complete. **Finish signing** remains unavailable until you have adopted a signature and completed the required text and checkbox fields.

## Step 4: Finish signing

Review the documents and click **Finish signing**. Your signature and field values are recorded, and you cannot revise them afterward.

If other signatures are still needed, CPAAutomation keeps the envelope active and, for sequential requests, advances to the next signing group. After the final signer submits, CPAAutomation prepares and digitally seals the completed PDF in the background. Participants receive a completion notification when that process finishes.

## Decline an envelope

You can click **Decline** at any point in the signing session. Enter a reason and confirm the decision.

Declining ends the envelope for every participant, not just for you. The sender is notified and can see the reason in the recipient timeline and audit trail. A declined envelope cannot be resumed; the sender must create a new envelope if the signing process should restart.

> **Note:** If an envelope expires or the sender voids it before you finish, it is no longer available for signing.
