description: "Track edits with accept/reject, and add, resolve, and manage comments — all from the editor's Review tab."

Inkwise includes a single-user review workflow for marking edits and leaving notes as you revise. You turn it on in the editor toolbar and manage everything from the **Review** tab in the editor's right sidebar.

## Track changes

**Turn it on.** Flip the **Track changes** switch on the right of the editor toolbar. While it's on, your insertions and deletions are marked instead of applied silently — the **Review** tab shows *New edits are being marked* (or *Turn on track changes in the editor toolbar to mark edits* when it's off).

**Review the changes.** Open the **Review** tab to see every pending change and a count of how many are outstanding. For each change you'll see:

- Whether it's an **Insertion** or **Deletion**, and when it was made.
- **Jump to change** to move the editor to that spot.
- The affected text.
- **Accept** to keep the change or **Reject** to undo it.

To clear everything at once, use **Accept all** or **Reject all** at the top of the list. When there's nothing outstanding, the tab shows *No tracked changes yet.*

## Comments

**Add a comment.** Select the text you want to annotate, then click the **Comment** action in the editor toolbar, type your note, and click **Save comment**. (The Comment action is available only when text is selected.)

**Manage comments.** The **Review** tab lists your comments with a count of how many are open. Each comment shows:

- Whether it's an **Open comment** or **Resolved comment**, and when it was created.
- **Jump to text** to move the editor to the commented passage.
- The comment body and the quoted text it refers to.
- **Resolve** to mark it done (or **Reopen** to bring it back), and **Delete** to remove it.

When there are no comments, the tab shows *No comments yet.*
